While I read some amazing books above, I can never capture everything, so some honorable mentions, and of course browse the shelf for more!

## Honorable Mentions

- **[Pale Fire](/blog/2025/04/04/pale-fire/)** by Vladimir Nabokov — A poem wrapped in an introduction and commentary that are part of a clever metafictional piece of work. Touches on themes of grief and an absurd story that takes place in the commentary. 
- **Project Hail Mary** by Andy Weir — A lonely journey in space that gets into the essence of friendship, language and sacrifice in the face of existential threats.
- **The Fifth Season** by N.K. Jemisin — Second person narration of a story across a broken earth, highlighting systemic oppersion and the will to survive. 
- **[Mr. President](/blog/2025/03/01/mr-president-penguin-classics/)** by Miguel Ángel Asturias — Written in 1946, its the template for novels about corrupt political leadership.
- **Howling Dark** by Christopher Ruocchio — The second book in the Sun Eater series, following **Empire of Silence**'s groundwork, the stakes and world building really start to come into play here.
