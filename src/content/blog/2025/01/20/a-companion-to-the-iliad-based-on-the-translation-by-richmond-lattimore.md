I found that while re-reading the Iliad, that I should flip to this copy of **A Companion to the Iliad** so that I can better understand what I was reading. 

In my case I didn't read the exact translation that this companion is meant to accompany, which is Richard Lattimore's translation. I read the translation of Caroline Alexander. This companion to the Iliad seemed to hold up well enough with her translation and made it very easy to follow. Highly recommended.
