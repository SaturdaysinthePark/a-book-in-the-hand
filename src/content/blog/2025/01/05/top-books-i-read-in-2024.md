As 2024 comes to an end, I'm reflecting on the books that made this year special. Here are my top 5 reads that earned the highest ratings and stayed with me long after I turned the final page.

## Honorable Mentions

These were all amazing reads over the year, just want to highlight them:

- **[The Stormlight Archive (Books 1–5)](/blog/2024/05/08/the-way-of-kings-the-stormlight-archive-1/)** by Brandon Sanderson — This epic fantasy series dominated my reading year. From *The Way of Kings*' masterful worldbuilding to *Wind and Truth*'s emotional conclusion, the scope feels genuinely epic.
- **The Anxious Generation** by Jonathan Haidt — was a big fan of his book The Righteous Mind, and this book was no exception to his thoughtful research and presentation into how smartphone culture has reshaped childhood with an eye-opening and urgent call to action. Essential reading for our current moment.
- **Red Rising Saga (Books 4–6)** by Pierce Brown — *Iron Gold* took a little getting used to, but then *Dark Age* was brutally emotional, and *Light Bringer* delivered an epic set up for the eventual finale, this series is terrific and a ton of fun.
- **[The Bright Sword](/blog/2024/08/27/the-bright-sword/)** by Lev Grossman — A fresh and interesting take on Arthurian legend, with a clever framing device and some unforgetable knights I had not spent as much time on learning about in the past.
- **Outlive** by Peter Attia — Terrific evidence based approach to longevity shifted my entire perspective on health and aging.
