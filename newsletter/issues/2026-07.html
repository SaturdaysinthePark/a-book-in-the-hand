Getting back into sharing what I read…
A note

Many of you have been following my reading journey over the years, and some consistently ask for an update or a recommendation. Well, I finally put it all in one place for you to explore at Saturdays in a Book. If you're getting this first newsletter, it's because you're a reader, a friend, someone from the present or past, and your contribution, suggestions and all else have kept me going throughout the years!

There's a lot of books I've reviewed, and many that I've read sitting on the shelf without a review, I promise to get to them! I'll also start to write some recommendations based on all my reading history! I hope it inspires or reminds you to read more!
